## LEGAL ANALYSIS

### Elements of Whistleblower Retaliation

#### 1. Protected Activity ✓
- Reported workplace safety concerns affecting patient care
- Shared factual performance data (41.23% failure rate)
- Communicated systemic issues to coworkers
- Activity protected under Maryland whistleblower statutes

#### 2. Adverse Employment Action ✓
- Final Written Warning (severe discipline)
- First discipline in 9-month employment history
- Highest disciplinary level before termination
- Creates permanent employment record

#### 3. Causal Connection ✓
- **Temporal Proximity:** 5 days between protected activity and discipline
- **Pattern of Censorship:** Four deletion demands preceding formal discipline
- **Disparate Treatment:** Same content praised by director, punished when shared
- **Direct Evidence:** Immediate censorship attempts after whistleblowing

#### 4. Pretext Analysis
- **Inconsistency:** Director acknowledged validity of concerns on Sept 29
- **Timing:** No discipline for 9 months, then immediate after whistleblowing
- **Severity:** Jumped to Final Warning without progressive discipline
- **Content:** Disciplined for sharing same analysis management requested

2. **Pattern:** Four censorship attempts in 8 hours followed by formal discipline

The evidence demonstrates a textbook case of retaliation for protected whistleblowing activity.
